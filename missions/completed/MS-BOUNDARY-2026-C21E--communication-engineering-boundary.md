# Mission

## Objective

Determine whether “Communication Engineering” contributes a distinct,
decision-useful object, method, or predictive theory beyond adjacent fields.

## Initial hypotheses

- H1: It is only a relabeling of existing fields.
- H2: Its value is an integrative engineering lifecycle for action-bearing
  communication artifacts, not a new basic science.
- H3: It has a unique causal theory or method not covered elsewhere.

## Required evidence

Foundational definitions, current standards, comparative method map, attempted
counterexamples, and at least one discriminating prediction.

Execution status is maintained in
`research/roadmaps/communication-engineering-body-of-work-analysis.md`. A body
of work is complete only when it satisfies that roadmap's completion gate.

## Success criteria

A boundary statement that names unique contribution and exclusions, or a
documented rejection of the discipline label.

## Stop conditions

Stop when a unique, testable contribution is established or when further search
only finds renamed adjacent-field constructs.

## Completion

Completed 2026-07-29. Twelve bounded body-of-work maps found no exclusive object,
mechanism, method, or prediction. RP-BOUNDARY-2026-5A7C recommends rejecting the
distinct-discipline claim; DF-BOUNDARY-2026-3F72 records the review-stage
disposition. A comparative experiment remains optional implementation research,
not a prerequisite for rejecting unsupported novelty.
