# Mission

## Objective

Select one two-to-four-week communication problem that has evidence of value,
observable outcomes, bounded risk, and a falsifiable pilot.

## Scope

Compared task-handoff reconstruction, architecture explanation, newcomer
onboarding, and code-change rationale support. Product implementation was
excluded.

## Success criteria

One reversible pilot selection, explicit alternatives, acceptance measures,
counterevidence, and stopping rules.

## Verification

Completed through RP-HANDOFF-2026-A8E1 and DF-HANDOFF-2026-5C9B. The decision is
provisional until the pilot's acceptance test is preregistered.
