# Purpose

Determine whether the proposed “Communication Engineering” label identifies a
distinct and useful engineering profile, or merely renames established bodies
of knowledge. This roadmap is a mutable execution view, not a registry record
and not evidence by itself.

# Completion gate for every body of work

A stream may be marked complete only when it records:

- foundational constructs and theories;
- recent peer-reviewed or standards-based developments;
- at least one empirical or field result where available;
- competing interpretations and counterevidence;
- methods and outcome measures reusable by this repository;
- what the body already explains;
- what it does not explain;
- overlap with the proposed profile;
- at least one assumption or belief challenged;
- a discriminating prediction or experiment;
- confidence, limitations, and exact source provenance;
- ten nontrivial future research questions when the stream is treated as a
  completed topic.

# Shared claims to challenge

- [x] Communication failure is mainly an information-content problem.
      Rejected: coordination, trust, ownership, and authority can dominate.
- [x] More complete documentation necessarily improves outcomes.
      Rejected: relevance and managed selectivity matter.
- [x] Communication quality can be evaluated independently of recipient,
      purpose, authority, timing, and organizational context. Rejected by
      expertise reversal, interruption moderators, and organizational mediation.
- [x] Tacit knowledge can usually be converted into an artifact. Rejected:
      transactive memory and knowledge integration remain social.
- [x] Reduced clarification means better communication.
      Rejected: questions can be productive coordination; silence can be
      disengagement.
- [x] A technically correct message causes correct action. Rejected:
      rhetorical framing, authority, closed-loop confirmation, and context
      mediate action.
- [x] A reusable template works across expertise levels and risk classes.
      Rejected by expertise reversal; adaptation must itself be tested.
- [x] AI summarization reduces total communication cost. Rejected as a general
      claim: factuality, verification, omission, and calibration costs apply.
- [x] Integration of existing methods constitutes a new discipline. Rejected:
      incremental predictive or practical value must be demonstrated.
- [x] The word “engineering” is justified without predictive or verification
      capability. Rejected: systems and decision science already provide the
      relevant burden of proof.

# Synthesis gate

After all streams:

- [x] Build a claim-by-field coverage matrix. See RP-BOUNDARY-2026-5A7C.
- [x] Identify any exclusive object, mechanism, method, or prediction. None found.
- [ ] Compare an integrated profile against the strongest adjacent method.
- [ ] Quantify added value and added process cost.
- [ ] Test whether the label improves retrieval and decisions or creates
      terminological confusion.
- [x] Accept, narrow, rename, or reject the proposed profile through a Decision
      Record. DF-BOUNDARY-2026-3F72 rejects the discipline claim and leaves only
      an experimental profile.

The three unchecked items are empirical follow-ups, not incomplete literature
streams. They require a preregistered experiment or terminology study and must
not be checked from synthesis alone.

# Stop and continuation rules

Stop a stream when its completion gate is met or when access limitations are
recorded and the remaining evidence cannot change the current decision. Never
mark a literature search exhaustive unless a reproducible systematic protocol
was actually run. Every incomplete stream must state the next source class,
query, or experiment needed.
