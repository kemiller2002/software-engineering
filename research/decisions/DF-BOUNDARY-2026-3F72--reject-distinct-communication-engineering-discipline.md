# Decision Record

## Context

The project charter prohibited assuming the discipline boundary. Twelve adjacent
bodies were mapped against the proposed capabilities.

## Decision

Reject “Communication Engineering” as a distinct scientific or engineering
discipline at review stage. Permit “experimental software
communication-assurance profile” as a noncanonical project description only
when it explicitly means a combination of existing methods and remains subject
to comparative validation.

## Evidence

RP-BOUNDARY-2026-5A7C and its claim-by-field coverage matrix. No exclusive
object, mechanism, method, or prediction was found.

## Alternatives

- Accept a new discipline: rejected as unsupported.
- Accept an established integration profile immediately: rejected because no
  incremental outcome or cost evidence exists.
- Abandon all integration work: rejected because a lightweight profile may
  still reduce method-selection cost.

## Consequences

Repository language must not imply disciplinary validation. Research should
target incremental utility, not novelty. Existing field terminology should be
used for individual methods.

## Reversibility and revisit trigger

Reversible. Revisit only if a replicated comparative study shows an exclusive
mechanism or meaningful incremental outcome that cannot be explained by the
component fields.
