# Theory

## Theory statement

Continuation quality improves when the task-relevant parts of a worker's mental
model are externalized in a recipient- and goal-specific artifact whose claims
are linked to inspectable evidence.

## Boundary conditions

This predicts bounded task continuation, not belonging, tacit skill transfer,
or general onboarding success.

## Alternative explanations

Template compliance may proxy author diligence; benefits may come from forcing
authors to think rather than from the artifact received.

## Confidence rationale

Low (0.38): the problem is evidenced, but the mechanism has not been tested.
