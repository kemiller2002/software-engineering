# Experiment

## Research question

Does a task-targeted structured handoff improve correct continuation relative to
an unstructured status note?

## Method

Use matched completed repository tasks. Blind successors to condition and ask
them to produce a continuation plan and execute one safe verification step.
Counterbalance task/condition assignment. Independent reviewers score plans
against concealed source records.

## Acceptance criteria

Pre-register a composite primary outcome: correct-plan time, material omissions,
unsafe assumptions, clarification questions, and handoff authoring cost.

## Falsification criteria

No practically meaningful improvement, increased unsafe confidence, or total
authoring cost exceeding reconstruction savings.

## Threats to validity

Template familiarity, task difficulty, reviewer subjectivity, artificial time
pressure, learning effects, and contamination.

## Results

Not run.
