# Research Journal Entry

## Objective

Select the first measurable communication vertical slice.

## Starting state

No slice or baseline was selected. The AI field experiment was not executable
without a partner, preregistration, and privacy-reviewed telemetry.

## Actions taken

Compared four candidates against evidence strength, measurability, two-to-four
week feasibility, privacy/safety, ROS relevance, and falsifiability.

## Observations

Handoff reconstruction has a directly evidenced information-availability
problem and can be tested with synthetic/public records. Onboarding and
architecture explanation are valuable but broader and more audience-dependent.

## Attempts to falsify

EV-HANDOFF-2026-B6E8 shows that task reconstruction cannot stand in for
onboarding. Authoring cost and false confidence are included as adverse outcomes.

## Decision and rationale

Selected a handoff reconstruction evaluator provisionally in
DF-HANDOFF-2026-5C9B.

## Failures and dead ends

No direct RCT of the proposed handoff schema was found in this rapid map.

## Highest-value next step

Preregister EX-HANDOFF-2026-0D7A and create a small, blinded evaluation corpus.
