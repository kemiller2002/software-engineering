# Research Journal Entry

## Objective

Define outcomes that detect useful handoff improvement without rewarding speed,
documentation volume, silence, or confident error.

## Alternatives considered

- Reconstruction time alone: rejected for construct underrepresentation.
- Reviewer-rated handoff quality: rejected as primary because it measures the
  artifact rather than recipient action.
- Clarification count: rejected because questions can be productive.
- One weighted composite: rejected until weights and construct behavior are
  validated.
- Correct execution alone: too costly and confounded for the first controlled
  study, but retained as validation evidence.

## Result

Use safe continuation success under a predeclared accessible time policy as the
primary outcome, with an explicit harm non-inferiority gate and separate
efficiency, calibration, coordination, experience, and lifecycle-cost measures.

The later safeguard analysis invalidated the assumption that one identical
clock is accessibility-neutral. A predeclared accommodation must apply in both
conditions, and elapsed time remains secondary.

## Highest-value next step

Complete safeguards and baseline missions, then preregister scoring examples,
reviewer training, reliability thresholds, and analysis.
