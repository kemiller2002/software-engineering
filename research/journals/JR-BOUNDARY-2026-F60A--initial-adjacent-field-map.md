# Research Journal Entry

## Objective

Start testing whether Communication Engineering adds a unique boundary.

## Actions taken

Mapped five adjacent foundations: information transmission, human-centred
design, systems integration, information architecture, and rhetoric.

## Observations

Nearly every broad candidate claim is already owned: fidelity by information
theory; audience/action/context by rhetoric; findability by IA; user-centred
lifecycle by HCI; integration and lifecycle by systems engineering.

## Failed assumptions

“Integrative” or “communication that leads to action” is not sufficient novelty.

## Highest-value next step

Map technical communication, CSCW/coordination, requirements engineering, and
safety-critical communication; then design a comparative test of incremental
utility.
