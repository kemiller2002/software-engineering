# Research Journal Entry

## Objective

Test whether current evidence supports a universal productivity claim for AI
coding assistance.

## Starting state

All 39 repository Markdown files were read. No accepted domain evidence,
hypothesis, theory, experiment, or REP existed. Baseline registry and validation
checks passed.

## Actions taken

Selected a bounded first topic; searched recent primary research; compared a
vendor controlled experiment, independent realistic RCT, organizational study,
holistic evaluation, and methodological update; created claim-level records.

## Observations

Effect estimates reverse across settings. Perception can diverge from measured
time. Individual and delivery-level outcomes can diverge. Automated correctness
does not guarantee merge readiness. Ubiquitous adoption makes abstention
experiments harder.

## Evidence collected

EV-AICODING-2026-10A1, EV-AICODING-2026-3B8F,
EV-AICODING-2026-6E21, EV-AICODING-2026-C940, and
EV-AICODING-2026-F5D2.

## Hypotheses considered

Universal positive effect; universal negative effect; time-only explanation;
conditional net-value effect.

## Attempts to falsify

Used the strongest positive controlled result against a universal-negative
claim and the strongest negative realistic result against a universal-positive
claim. Neither universal claim survived its boundary conditions.

## Decisions and rationale

Adopted HY-AICODING-2026-7C2D as supported and TH-AICODING-2026-44D0 as a
candidate, both at Medium confidence. No practitioner deployment mandate was
issued.

## Failures and dead ends

This was not a systematic review. Full-text extraction was incomplete for the
DORA report. Studies are not sufficiently harmonized for meta-analysis.

## Confidence changes

From no justified belief to Medium confidence that effects are heterogeneous;
Low-to-Medium confidence in any named moderator's causal contribution.

## Files changed

Mission, five evidence records, hypothesis, theory, proposed experiment,
journal, REP, current context, queue, and handoff.

## Highest-value next step

Review the REP, preregister EX-AICODING-2026-2D77 with a participating
organization, then run a telemetry pilot.
