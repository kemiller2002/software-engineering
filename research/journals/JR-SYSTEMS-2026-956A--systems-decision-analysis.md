# Systems Engineering and Decision Science Analysis

## Model and evidence

Systems engineering owns lifecycle integration and interfaces
[EV-BOUNDARY-2026-5E8B]. Assurance cases link claims, arguments, and evidence
[EV-SYSTEMS-2026-22C8]. Value-of-information analysis measures expected decision
improvement, not information volume [EV-SYSTEMS-2026-A730].

## Counterevidence and methods

Assurance cases can become persuasive paperwork; evidence quality is not assured
by structure. Quantitative decision models require contentious probabilities and
utilities. Methods include interface analysis, traceability, claims-argument-
evidence review, uncertainty/sensitivity analysis, VoI, and verification plans.

## Coverage, gap, and challenged assumptions

This body already owns integration, lifecycle, risk, evidence arguments, and
decision value. It most directly challenges the need for a new “engineering”
field. Its communication methods may be too heavyweight for routine work.

## Discriminating experiment

Compare a lightweight assurance-case handoff with the proposed profile,
measuring decision accuracy, evidence gaps, authoring cost, and update cost.
