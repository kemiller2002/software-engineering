# Evidence Record

## Evidence summary

DORA reports that heavier AI use correlates with better self-reported flow,
satisfaction, and productivity while a 25% adoption increase associates with
1.5% lower delivery throughput and 7.2% lower delivery stability.

## Exact claim supported or contradicted

Individual experience and system-level delivery performance can move in
opposite directions.

## Source provenance

Primary organizational research report using large-scale survey/interview
methods; this record relies on the publisher's public summary.

## Relevant excerpt or data

Reported associations: +25% adoption, -1.5% throughput, -7.2% stability.

## Interpretation

Local code generation gains may shift costs into batch size, review,
integration, or operations.

## Limitations

Observational associations do not establish causality; constructs and sample
composition require full-report review; self-report and common-method bias.

## Counterevidence

Controlled task evidence can show direct speed gains, but does not measure the
organizational outcome.

## Reproduction or verification notes

Test using repository telemetry with adoption timing, batch-size mediation,
quality outcomes, and matched controls.
