# Evidence Record

## Evidence summary

Interviews with 32 developers and 15 managers, triangulated by surveys of 189
developers and 37 managers, found that onboarding tasks affect learning,
confidence, and socialization and require situation-dependent strategies.

## Interpretation

A reconstruction-time pilot is too narrow to claim improved onboarding. It can
test task continuation only.

## Limitations

Single-company case study and self-reported outcomes; nevertheless it is strong
counterevidence against collapsing onboarding into document comprehension.
