# Evidence Record

Of 174 ambiguity studies mapped from 1995–2015, only 28 had empirical
evaluation; 81% focused on detection and very few on reduction or removal. The
field supplies constructs and tools but also demonstrates the danger of
equating detection with improved engineering outcomes.
