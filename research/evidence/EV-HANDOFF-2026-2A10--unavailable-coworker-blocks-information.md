# Evidence Record

## Evidence summary

Observation of 17 developers identified 21 information types. Design rationale,
intended behavior, and causes of program state were often deferred; tasks
stalled when the only source was an unavailable coworker.

## Interpretation

A handoff artifact should expose rationale, intended behavior, current state,
and unresolved causes rather than merely list files changed.

## Limitations

One collocated organization and older tooling; the study does not test a
handoff intervention.
