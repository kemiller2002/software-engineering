# Evidence Record

## Evidence summary

DORA operationalized documentation through clarity, findability, reliability,
accuracy, currency, and goal support, and reports associations between stronger
documentation and organizational/technical capability outcomes.

## Interpretation

The pilot should score handoffs on goal completion, clarity, findability, and
reliability—not presence or word count.

## Limitations

Organization-level observational modeling does not prove that one handoff
artifact causes performance improvement.
