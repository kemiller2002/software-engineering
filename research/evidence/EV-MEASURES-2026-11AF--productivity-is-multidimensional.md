# Evidence Record

SPACE argues that developer productivity spans satisfaction, performance,
activity, communication/collaboration, and efficiency/flow and cannot be reduced
to one metric. For handoffs, elapsed reconstruction time cannot stand alone for
correctness, safety, coordination, or experience.

This record constrains measurement design. It neither confirms nor contradicts
whether the proposed structured handoff causes better continuation.

## Limitations

SPACE is a framework, not validation of this repository's proposed outcome.
