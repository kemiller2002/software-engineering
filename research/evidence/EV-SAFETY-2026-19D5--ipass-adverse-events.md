# Evidence Record

Across 3,744 clinical handoffs, the composite event rate was 6.35% under usual
care and 3.61% with structured I-PASS, a reported 43% relative reduction.
Transfer to software is unproven and the intervention includes verbal synthesis
and read-back, not a document template alone.
