# Evidence Record

Crossover designs are common in software-engineering experiments because each
participant can serve as a control, reducing subject variability and sample
requirements. They also introduce period, learning, and treatment carryover
effects. Exposure to a structured handoff can teach a participant which
questions to ask in a later control task, so counterbalancing alone cannot
guarantee an uncontaminated comparison.

## Limitations

The paper provides design guidance rather than data about this intervention.
Whether carryover is material must be tested in a pilot.
