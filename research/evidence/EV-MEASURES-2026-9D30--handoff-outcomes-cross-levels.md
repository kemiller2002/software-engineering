# Evidence Record

Handoff meta-analysis distinguishes information transferred, patient outcomes,
provider outcomes, and organizational outcomes and examines protocol length as
a moderator. An intervention can improve content transfer without improving the
ultimate system outcome.

This record supports a multilevel outcome model, not the causal claim that this
repository's proposed handoff intervention works.

## Limitations

Clinical effects do not transfer directly to software; the measurement logic is
the relevant evidence.
