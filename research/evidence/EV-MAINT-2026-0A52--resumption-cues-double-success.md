# Evidence Record

A survey of 371 programmers plus a controlled study found automated contextual
cues produced twice the task-completion success of note-taking alone.
Chronological code-snippet cues were preferred. This directly supports testing
state-reconstruction aids, while lab transfer and tool age remain limits.
