# Evidence Record

WCAG 2.2 requires operable content, keyboard access, and enough time, including
adjustable timing where a limit is not essential. It also notes that technical
conformance does not address every user need. The experiment's identical fixed
window is therefore not accessibility-neutral.

The protocol should provide accessible materials and tools, test keyboard and
assistive-technology paths, permit predeclared timing accommodations, and keep
elapsed time secondary. A participant should not need to disclose a diagnosis
to request an accommodation.

## Limitations

WCAG governs web content, while the study may also use editors, terminals, and
documents. Participatory testing remains necessary; conformance is not proof of
equivalent research participation.
