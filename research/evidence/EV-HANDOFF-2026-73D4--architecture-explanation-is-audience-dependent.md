# Evidence Record

## Evidence summary

Interviews with nine architecture explainers and eight recipients found that
useful explanations balance problem and technical domains and vary scope/detail
with the recipient's role, experience, and goal.

## Interpretation

Handoff quality cannot be evaluated without a declared successor and next task.

## Limitations

Exploratory interview study, small sample, and no causal outcome test.
