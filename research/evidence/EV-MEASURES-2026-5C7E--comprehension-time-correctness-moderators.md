# Evidence Record

An empirical study with 44 developers measured reading time, identification of
overall purpose, and ability to extend tests. Project knowledge and experience
affected outcomes. This supports separating efficiency, understanding, and
action while stratifying recipient expertise.

## Limitations

Test-code comprehension is narrower than repository-task handoff.
